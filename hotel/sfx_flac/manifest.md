# When It Goes Dark: "Winter Storm: 12 Hours Trapped in a Hotel With No Power", sound effects

All 23 files from the brief's Sound list are in this folder: WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM.
- Loops (`amb_*`, `sfx_generator_hum`, plus loop-safe `sfx_heartbeat`, `sfx_cat_purr`) wrap without clicks. The synthesized loops are built periodically, so the last sample flows straight into the first. The purr is end-crossfaded.
- One-shots are trimmed, edge-faded and peak-normalized to -6 dBFS. Two are set lower on purpose: `sfx_generator_off` is level-matched to the hum loop, and `sfx_phone_ring` is a steady tone. Beds and loops (`amb_*`, `sfx_generator_hum`) are at -24 LUFS integrated, with true peaks at or below -3.5 dBTP.
- Nothing contains music or voices. The only voiced sounds are the cat (F0 around 600-800 Hz).
- Measurements below come from ffmpeg: astats sample peak, ebur128 true peak (TP) and integrated loudness.

Sources: **reused** = Season 1 ElevenLabs flow rJlIUOf7hVqeignE5TBe. **generated** = new ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2 takes on flow QeuEeH3kk0H34V1qdxRD ("WIGD Hotel SFX"). **synthesized** = numpy/scipy (scripts in the session scratchpad).
ElevenLabs spend: 404 credits (32 takes across 13 prompts, per the runs' reported credit_costs; the pre-run estimate was 1,320). Cap was 3,000.

## Hand-off notes
- Generator chain: `sfx_generator_start` ends in the hum loop's exact timbre at about the loop's level. Crossfade into `sfx_generator_hum` over the start's last ~0.5 s. Cut to `sfx_generator_off` at its native level (its first 1.2 s is the same hum), then let "The silence is enormous" play over its tail.
- Scratching pans left to right. Flip it in the edit if the walk goes the other way.
- Season 1 storm loop not used: it is a rain/thunder hurricane bed, wrong for snow. S1 power-down not used: it has a hiss floor. S1 click and whoosh not needed.
- Takes were chosen from spectrograms and measurements, not by ear. Give each a quick listen when assembling, especially the cat sounds.
