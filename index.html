    About the show

    Made in hurricane country

    When It Goes Dark is made in South Florida, where losing power is a yearly event. Every episode is a story you live through, and every episode teaches one real survival fact from Ready.gov, the CDC or the USDA, with the source in the description.

    The art and narration are made with AI tools. The real Lando is an orange cat who is, in fact, not helping.
